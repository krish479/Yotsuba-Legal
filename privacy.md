# Yotsuba Nakano Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

## Information We Store

Depending on which features are used, Yotsuba Nakano may store Discord user IDs, server IDs, virtual currency balances, experience and level information, transaction records, and relationship or family-tree selections.

## Why We Use This Information

This information is used to provide bot features, maintain balances and levels, process transactions, and display relationship or profile information within the relevant Discord server.

## Data Storage

Bot data is stored in the database maintained by the bot operator. Data is used to provide the bot's features and is not intended to be sold.

## Third-Party Services

The bot may use external services to retrieve anime GIFs. Those services may process requests made to retrieve content according to their own policies.

## Data Questions and Requests

For questions about stored data or requests for assistance, contact the bot developer through the official support server.

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.
